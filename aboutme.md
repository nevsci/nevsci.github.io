#### Career Overview Summary

- **2023–2026:** Postdoctoral Researcher, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin  
  *Group of Prof. Stefan Hecht*  
  - Developement of novel spiropyran-based photoswitching systems for Xolography and broader applications  
  - Expansion of the spiropyran chemical space using machine learning 
  - Preparation and contribution to grant applications 

- **2022–2023:** Postdoctoral Researcher, Universität Münster  
  *Group of Prof. Ryan Gilmour*  
  - Collaboration with DSM Vitamin 
  - Advanced olefin manipulations applicable in vitamin synthesis 

- **2018–2022:** PhD Student, Universität Münster  
  *Group of Prof. Ryan Gilmour*  
  - Design of new photochemical reactions using triplet sensitization and ConPET 
  - Exploration reaction mechanisms with probes, spectroscopy and calculations 
  - Supervision Master’s and Bachelor’s students 

- **2017–2018:** Research Assistant, Universität Regensburg  
  *Group of Prof. Burkhard König*  
  - Design and characterized novel flavin-based photocatalysts suitable for demanding reductive processes 

- **2011–2018:** Undergraduate Researcher, University of Chemistry and Technology in Prague  
  *Group of Prof. Radek Cibulka*  
  - Developement of systems for photochemical oxidation of sulfides 
  - Optimization of reaction conditions , mechanism study and and application to biologically relevant substances
